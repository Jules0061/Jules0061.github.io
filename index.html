Project Overview

CloneDupe is a Minecraft server i originally created back in December 2023. It quickly became a big success before it was raided, which led to it shutting down for around 6 months. I decided to bring it back because i've always enjoyed developing it, and it's always been a dream of mine to build it into a large and successful Minecraft server. That re-development is now finished, and CloneDupe has since been released. The server is currently open and online, so anyone can join and play.

It is one of my major Minecraft server projects and the one i am most involved with. Alongside the day to day server work, i build and maintain the custom plugins that run on it. With CloneDupe open to players, my focus is on keeping it stable, optimized and updated.
